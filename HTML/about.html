Growing Together.

Chuka University Scouting Club is a student led scouting community that brings young people together through leadership, service, outdoor adventure and friendship.

We embrace the spirit of Scouting by encouraging personal growth, responsibility and meaningful participation in the community.

Our Story

A Journey Defined by Purpose.

Scouting is more than wearing a uniform. It is a commitment to personal development, serving others and becoming a responsible member of society.

At Chuka University, we carry this spirit into university life, creating opportunities to learn, work together and make a positive contribution beyond the classroom.

Purpose in Every Step. +

Our Mission

To contribute to the education of young people through a value system based on the Scout Promise and Law, helping build a better world where people are self fulfilled as individuals and play a constructive role in society.

Our Vision

The Vision of the Kenya Scouts Association is to be the leading youth movement in Kenya, developing well rounded citizens who are agents of positive change.
